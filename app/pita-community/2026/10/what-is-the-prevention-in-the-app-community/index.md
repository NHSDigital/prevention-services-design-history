There's a lot happening across prevention services in the NHS app.

Teams are developing new services, improving existing journeys, researching user needs and solving difficult problems. But when we're heads-down delivering our own work, it's not always easy to see the bigger picture.

We might be tackling similar challenges, learning things that would be useful to others, or working on different parts of the same user journey without realising it.

That's why we've started the Prevention in the app community.

## What is the community?

Prevention in the app is a new community bringing together colleagues across the Digital Prevention Service Portfolio and Product & Platforms who are delivering prevention-related services, journeys and capabilities through the NHS App.

It's a space to step back from our individual work, understand what others are doing and find opportunities to work together.

We want the community to help us:

- **Look sideways:** discover what other teams are working on and what's coming next.
- **Connect:** find colleagues working on related problems, services or user needs.
- **Reuse:** share research, evidence, design patterns and approaches rather than starting from scratch.
- **Unblock:** surface challenges affecting multiple teams and find people who can help resolve them.

## Why does this matter?

People don't experience NHS services as separate products owned by different teams. They want joined-up experiences that help them understand their health and take action.

As the NHS app evolves towards becoming a companion for people's health, making those connections between services becomes increasingly important. And that means making better connections between the people designing and delivering them.

## Follow along

We'll share what we learn through the community in this blog.

Sometimes the most useful thing we can do is look up from our own work and see what someone else is doing.
